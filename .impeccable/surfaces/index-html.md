# Surface: home (index.html)

Scope: the whole personal site, one page. Visitor mode: Experience. Audience and roster live in PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a LAN party where every project has a seat and the bots are the players. It refuses the dark developer portfolio of a name block over a grid of screenshot cards.

OWN-WORLD: A dark room (#111319) lit only by screens. Folding-table bands in warm gray laminate. Masking tape (#e6d5a8) carries every project name in marker. Printed sheets (#f2efe6, ink #17171a) are taped to the wall and beside each seat; they hold all prose. One red (#e2513b) marks the seat in use. Archivo for printed sheets and big lines, a marker face only on tape, mono only for seat numbers and specs.

STORY: A visitor reads the sign-in sheet, sees bots playing real games on live screens, walks the tables, and leaves knowing what each project is and where to look further.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left third, the sign-in sheet taped slightly askew: RAFE SYMONDS huge, one line, the seat list as navigation. Right two thirds, the first two seats on a table: Battlesnake and Screeps screens running, tape labels, one huge line each. GitHub and LinkedIn on the sheet.

FORM: LAN Party, position 6 of 7 on the grounded list. seed:eab6fe5002703487

Raises: identical seat unit (VU bridge). Only the seat in view lights up (hand-processed film). One shared tick drives every screen (paper automata). One huge line per seat (linocut). One continuous room, not boxed sections (ebru).

Signature interaction: screens power up as their seat comes into view and dim when it leaves, all ticking on one clock. Sign-in rows light their seat on hover and jump to it on click.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
